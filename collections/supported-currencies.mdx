Tabs Connect supports collections in 10 currencies, grouped into African local currencies, G3 currencies, and stablecoins. Use this page to confirm which currencies your global accounts can receive funds in.

## African currencies

Collect funds locally across major African markets.

| Currency | Code | Country |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ghanaian Cedi | GHS | Ghana |
| Nigerian Naira | NGN | Nigeria |
| Central African CFA Franc | XAF | CEMAC region |
| West African CFA Franc | XOF | WAEMU region |
| Kenyan Shilling | KES | Kenya |

## G3 currencies

Collect in the world's major reserve currencies.

| Currency | Code | Region |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US Dollar | USD | United States |
| Euro | EUR | Eurozone |
| British Pound | GBP | United Kingdom |

## Stablecoins

Collect in USD-pegged stablecoins on supported blockchains.

| Currency | Code |
| --- | --- |
| Tether | USDT |
| USD Coin | USDC |

<Note>
  All 10 currencies are live and available for collections through your Tabs Connect global accounts.
</Note>
