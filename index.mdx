Tabs Connect is a global money movement platform for businesses. Issue named global accounts to collect funds from your customers and counterparties, then pay out across 10 currencies to over 80 countries, all from a single integration.

## What you can do with Tabs Connect

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Issue global accounts" icon="building-columns">
    Provide your customers and counterparties with named accounts in major currencies to receive funds locally.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Collect from anywhere" icon="download">
    Accept incoming payments from customers and counterparties around the world into your global accounts.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Pay out in 10 currencies" icon="money-bill-transfer">
    Send funds in the local currency of your recipient across a wide range of supported corridors.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Reach 80+ countries" icon="globe">
    Move money to recipients in more than 80 countries through a single API and dashboard.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Who it's for

Tabs Connect is built for businesses that move money across borders at scale, including:

- Marketplaces paying out sellers, creators, and service providers
- Payroll and contractor platforms disbursing wages globally
- Financial institutions and fintechs offering multi-currency accounts to their users
- Import, export, and B2B businesses collecting from and paying counterparties abroad

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create global accounts">
    Issue named collection accounts in supported currencies for your business, customers, or counterparties.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect incoming funds">
    Share account details with payers so they can send funds locally, without international transfer friction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay out globally">
    Initiate payouts in any of 10 supported currencies to recipients in 80\+ countries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reconcile and report">
    Track balances, transactions, and payout status across every currency in one place.
  </Step>
</Steps>
